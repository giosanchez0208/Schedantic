**What this is:** the harvest tells us which constructions real schedule text actually contains. Where the count is zero or near-zero, either the construction is genuinely rare *or* the harvest's register cannot see it — the two look identical in the data and are distinguished in the notes.

**What this is not:** a measurement of the target distribution. Parser test suites over-represent absolute dates because that is what they test; forum posts under-represent attendees. Treat every number as directional.
